# Three Global Benchmarks, Four Project Pillars & Signature Amenities

## Three Global Benchmarks (*Pre-Certification Aligned)
The Eastern Chambers brings together three prestigious international standards into one future-ready commercial building:
1. **LEED Gold* (Sustainability & Environmental Performance):**
   - Global benchmark for environmental performance across energy efficiency, water conservation, indoor environmental quality, sustainable materials, and site considerations.
2. **WiredScore Gold* (Digital Connectivity & Resilience):**
   - Global benchmark for digital connectivity, telecom resilience, fiber readiness, and technology infrastructure helping businesses stay seamlessly connected.
3. **SmartScore Gold* (Smart Building & Automation):**
   - Global benchmark for smart building technology, automation, IoT integration, and data-enabled building operations.

## The Four Core Project Pillars

### Pillar 01: Intelligence ("Intelligence behind tomorrow's workplace")
Technology is seamlessly integrated into the building to make everyday operations connected, responsive, and effortless:
- **SmartScore Gold & WiredScore Gold*** (*Pre-Certification Aligned)
- **IoT-Enabled Tenant Experience**
- **IBMS Powered by Enlite** (Intelligent Building Management System)
- **Dedicated Tenant App** for digital access control, visitor management, and building services

### Pillar 02: Architecture ("Designed for the Future of Work")
Balances functionality, flexibility, and architectural distinction:
- **Triple-Side Views** — Mangroves, Mountains, and City Skyline
- **Stone, Metal and Glass Façade**
- **Biophilic Landscape**
- **Hospitality-Grade Double-Height Arrival Lobby with Café**
- **Column-Free Floor Plates** for high space efficiency
- **Eight High-Speed Elevators**
- **Natural Daylight on Every Side**

### Pillar 03: Sustainability ("Sustainability redefining future-ready workspaces")
Resource-efficient systems that lower operating costs and support corporate ESG goals:
- **LEED Gold*** (*Pre-Certification Aligned)
- **Rainwater Harvesting and Recycling**
- **On-Site Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)**
- **Solar Integration**
- **Double-Glazed Glass** for thermal and acoustic control
- **EV Charging Stations**

### Pillar 04: Wellness ("Wellness enabling next-gen office culture")
Natural light, greenery, cleaner air, and breakout zones to pause, connect, and recharge:
- **10,000 Sq.Ft. Dedicated Green Reserve**
- **Landscaped Rooftop with Breakout Zones**
- **Retail and Dining at the Base**
- **AQI (Air Quality Index) Infrastructure in Common Areas**
- **Reserved Indoor Amenity Space**

## Five Signature Amenity Zones
1. **01 / Green Reserve (Landscaped Reserve):** A 10,000 sq.ft. dedicated biophilic green reserve — a quiet place to pause outdoors.
2. **02 / Elevated Workspace (Rooftop Amenities):** Open-air rooftop work pods, garden, and recreation zones to work and unwind above the city.
3. **03 / Urban Court (Outdoor Recreation):** Rooftop outdoor racket/sports court surrounded by planting for a quick physical reset.
4. **04 / Twilight Terrace (Open-Air Terrace):** Covered terrace with seating and green lawn at dusk to meet, host events, and unwind in the open air.
5. **05 / Sitout Garden (Garden Sitout):** Landscaped garden with seating beside the building to connect and recharge.
